AELID OAK GROVE KIT
Layered foliage revision

Open Aelid-Oak-Grove.blend. Golden Grove is the assembled isometric
woodland. Oak Silhouettes shows the four trees side by side. Leaf Spray
Sources shows the four oak sprigs, and Leaf Atlas Bake shows the
orthographic layout used for the texture. Find the marked tree
collections and spray objects under Current File in the Asset Browser.

TREE MODELS

Oak_Broadcrown   A stout trunk under a broad, rounded canopy
Oak_Splitfork    A forked trunk and a gap between the upper crowns
Oak_Windlean     A leaning trunk with a swept, asymmetric canopy
Oak_Ancient      A spreading canopy with exposed roots and a bare snag

Each FBX in FBX/Trees contains a Bark and a LeafCards mesh, with a
ground-level pivot at the base of the trunk. Units are meters. Each
canopy is formed by 378 quads grouped along the branches and around
their ends. Smaller clusters soften the outline and leave gaps where
the trunk and limbs show through. The grove scene adds the trail,
flowers, stones, and lighting for the preview.

LEAF SPRAYS

Fan, Fork, Cascade, and Tip are branched sprigs with 30 individually
modeled oak leaves each. FBX/Sprays contains the four mesh exports,
with each pivot at the base of the twig.

Oak-Leaf-Spray-Atlas.png is a 2048 x 2048 RGBA image. The 2 x 2 tiles
read Fan, Fork, Cascade, Tip from upper left to lower right. The tree
cards already have the corresponding UVs. Blender also packs the atlas
into the project file.

UNITY SETUP

1. Import FBX/Trees and the atlas PNG. The four spray FBXs are available
   for creating new arrangements or rebuilding the atlas.
2. Assign LeafCards a double-sided, alpha-clipped foliage material.
   Use atlas RGB as the leaf color and alpha as the cutout. Inspect
   the cutoff at gameplay zoom with mipmaps enabled.
3. The vertex color red channel carries the wind weight. Roots have a
   weight of 0. Upper limbs and leaves flex as the weight increases.
   The weather system can drive the wind strength.
4. Apply the same cutout and vertex displacement to the color, shadow,
   depth, and ASCII surface passes that render the foliage.

The preview was rendered from the Blender assets. The FBXs were
reimported in Blender to verify bounds, UVs, and wind weights. Unity
shader wiring and in-game performance still need to be tested.
